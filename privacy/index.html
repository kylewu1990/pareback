Pareback Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026 ·

English中文

English

Pareback does not collect any data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it does not send your photos or any information about them to us or anyone else.

  • Photo library. Pareback asks for access to your photo library to find similar photos, screenshots, blurry photos and large videos. All analysis (image similarity, text recognition, face recognition, content labels) runs on your device using Apple's frameworks.
  • Data kept on your device. To avoid re-analyzing your library, Pareback stores analysis results (such as recognized text and face features) in the app's private storage on your iPhone. They are never uploaded and are removed when you delete the app.
  • iCloud Photos. If your library uses iCloud Photos, iOS may download originals from Apple's iCloud so Pareback can analyze or play them. This is handled by Apple, not by Pareback.
  • Apple Intelligence. On supported devices, search requests may be interpreted by Apple's on-device language model. Pareback does not use any server for this.
  • Deleting photos. Pareback only deletes items after you confirm in the iOS system prompt. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted and can be restored for 30 days.
  • Children. Pareback does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions: wukai1990@gmail.com

中文

Pareback 不收集任何数据。本 App 没有账号、没有统计分析、没有广告，也没有第三方 SDK，不会把你的照片或任何相关信息发送给我们或其他任何人。

  • 照片图库。Pareback 需要访问你的照片图库，用来找出相似照片、截屏、模糊照片和大视频。所有分析（相似度、文字识别、人脸识别、画面标签）都通过 Apple 的系统框架在你的设备上完成。
  • 保存在设备上的数据。为避免重复分析，Pareback 会把分析结果（如识别出的文字和人脸特征）保存在 App 自己的存储空间里，从不上传；删除 App 时一并清除。
  • iCloud 照片。如果你开启了 iCloud 照片，iOS 可能从 Apple 的 iCloud 下载原图，以便 Pareback 分析或播放。这一过程由 Apple 处理，与 Pareback 无关。
  • Apple 智能。在支持的设备上，搜索语句可能由 Apple 的本机语言模型理解，Pareback 不使用任何服务器。
  • 删除照片。Pareback 只会在你于系统弹窗中确认后删除项目；删除的项目进入“最近删除”，30 天内可以恢复。
  • 儿童。Pareback 不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的信息。

本政策如有更新，会发布在此页面。如有疑问请联系：wukai1990@gmail.com
